The rules

  1. World PvP only. Battlegrounds and duels don't count.
  2. Fair fights only: the victim can be at most 3 levels below you.
  3. Each victim counts once per day. Camping the same gnome ten times is not ten kills.
  4. Proof: a clip, or screenshots showing the kill message in chat with chat timestamps on. Several kills can go in one post. Pick the challenge on the form.
  5. In launch week a moderator checks every entry.
  6. Kill-trading with friends or alts is cheating and lands you in the Hall of Shame.
What you win

The first 3 hunters per faction to reach the target go into the Hall of Fame and get named in the first War Report. The faction with more challenge kills wins week 1 and gets bragging rights until the next weekly reset.
