WoW Forever week 1: the best player clips

World of Warcraft: Forever opened on 5 November 2026, and the first week was exactly what a fresh PvP server promises: ganks that went wrong, levelling in packs, and a few quiet moments worth a screenshot. These are the best clips and screenshots players sent to BigDForever in launch week, with the story behind each one.

1

Three levels higher, two on one, and he still thought he'd win

HordeThe BarrensWorld PvPClip: Light

A level 16 undead paladin and a level 13 friend run into a level 19 night elf in the Barrens. On paper the night elf has every advantage: three levels on the paladin, six on the friend. He takes the fight anyway. It does not go the way he planned. This was the first player kill sent to BigDForever, and our news anchors gave it the full broadcast treatment.

Raw clip: “Barrens Trash Cleanup Crew” on Medal.

2

Clip It and Ship It: the gnome rogue who picked the wrong grind spot

HordeSilverpine ForestWorld PvPGnomilationClip: Her Lightness

A level 23 gnome rogue spots a level 17 Horde character questing alone in Silverpine Forest and goes for the easy kill. Six levels up, stealth on his side, the opener is his. Then the fight keeps going, and going, and the gnome ends up as the Horde's entry for the week 1 Gnomilation bounty. Proof that a level gap is a promise, not a guarantee.

▶ Watch on Medal
3

Postcards from launch week: Tirisfal and Mulgore

HordeTirisfal GladesMulgoreMake Love Not WarBy Her Lightness

Not every brag needs a body count. Two screenshots from the first days: morning light breaking through the trees of Tirisfal Glades, and the long walk across Mulgore towards Thunder Bluff. Peaceful posts like these don't count for the faction war, but they compete for Postcard of the Week.

Also from the same week: Questing and Resting on Medal, an inn break between quests.

Week 1 bounties

Gnomilation and the Milk Run

Each week BigDForever runs one world PvP bounty per faction. In week 1 the Horde hunted gnome players (Gnomilation) and the Alliance hunted tauren (the Milk Run). Both end at the EU weekly reset on Wednesday 11 November, 05:00 Belgrade time (04:00 UTC). Week 2 brings new targets.
